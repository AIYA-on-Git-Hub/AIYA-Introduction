# AI Youth Alliance (AIYA) 🌐

Welcome to the official GitHub home of the **AI Youth Alliance (AIYA)**.

The **AI Youth Alliance is a global, student-led network dedicated to empowering students and schools to meet the generational challenge of AI.**

AIYA creates hands-on opportunities for students to **learn, lead, and innovate with AI** within their academic communities. Working with students, faculty, and administrative advisors, AIYA develops experiential programming that builds foundational AI literacy and fluency while helping students maintain agency, critical thinking, analytical capacity, and genuine authorship in an AI-driven world.

AIYA operates under the auspices of the **Hey AI! Foundation**.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

AIYA equips students with **foundational AI literacy and fluency in essential AI skills and techniques through experiential programming**, while advancing conversations around best practices for AI and learning at and between schools around the world.

We believe students should experience AI directly rather than simply learn about it as an abstract subject.

Through hands-on challenges, collaborative problem-solving, and practical engagement with AI, students develop the skills and judgment necessary to use these technologies effectively and safely.

---

## 🌎 Our Vision

AIYA seeks to **prepare students for success through direct engagement with AI within the structured learning environment of their schools.**

AI presents extraordinary opportunities to amplify human creativity, problem-solving, research, and access to knowledge. At the same time, uncritical reliance on AI can undermine critical thinking, analytical capacity, subject mastery, agency, and genuine authorship.

AIYA's vision is to build a **vibrant, self-sustaining, student-led community** that explores and advances best practices for using AI in learning.

We believe students should be equipped not simply to consume AI, but to **understand it, question it, build with it, and shape its role in their education and future.**

---

## 🧠 Experiential AI Education

AIYA's approach is fundamentally **experiential**.

Rather than relying on a fixed curriculum, AIYA provides students with structured opportunities to explore the capabilities and limitations of artificial intelligence through direct experience.

Students engage with AI through:

- Hands-on challenges and problem-solving
- AI literacy and fluency development
- Technical experimentation and building
- Collaborative projects
- Critical evaluation of AI systems
- Exploration of real-world applications
- Student-led research and creative work

The goal is to help students develop both the **technical fluency to use AI** and the **critical literacy to understand when, why, and how it should be used.**

---

## 🏫 AIYA in Schools

AIYA works with students and schools to bring experiential AI learning into the academic environment.

Students can participate through school-based AI clubs and communities, connect existing student organizations with the AIYA network, or establish an **AIYA Chapter** at their school.

AIYA's school-based model is designed to give students opportunities to:

- Develop foundational AI literacy
- Build practical AI fluency
- Explore AI through direct experience
- Collaborate with peers
- Develop and apply technical skills
- Solve meaningful problems
- Exercise agency in learning
- Preserve genuine authorship and analytical capacity

Faculty and administrative advisors help support AIYA's work within their school communities.

---

## 🌐 A Global Student-Led Network

AIYA connects students and schools around a shared goal: **helping young people navigate and shape an AI-driven world.**

The network supports student-led exploration of AI across disciplines and encourages students to learn from one another, collaborate on projects, and share emerging practices for effective and responsible AI use in education.

AIYA's community extends beyond individual schools through global programming, collaborative projects, open technical resources, and opportunities for students to connect with peers around the world.
